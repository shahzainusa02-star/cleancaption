# Third-Party Software

CleanCaption installs and uses third-party Python packages, including FastAPI, Uvicorn,
python-multipart, NumPy, and OpenCV. Those projects remain governed by their own licenses.

CleanCaption also requires FFmpeg to be installed separately by the user. FFmpeg is not
bundled in the CleanCaption release ZIP.

The CleanCaption proprietary license does not replace or restrict rights granted by any
third-party open-source license.
