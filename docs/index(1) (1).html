CleanCaption is a macOS utility for removing supported burned-in captions without uploading your video to a CleanCaption server.

Download for macOS Installation

Simple local workflow

Choose a video, remove the detected caption area, and export a compatible MP4 while preserving the original audio.

Local

Your selected video is processed on your own computer.

No API key

No Replicate, RunPod, or CleanCaption cloud account is required.

MP4 export

Exports H.264 video with AAC audio for broad playback compatibility.

Privacy by design

CleanCaption v1.0 Beta does not upload selected videos to a CleanCaption server. The first launch may download required Python packages.

macOS beta requirements

macOS, Python 3, and FFmpeg. On first launch, right-click Start CleanCaption.command and choose Open.

Current beta limitation: optimized for centered white captions with a dark outline in the lower-middle area of portrait videos. Other caption styles or locations may not work correctly yet.
